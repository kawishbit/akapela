# Audio only, no video playback

Every feature except plain YouTube playback needs the raw audio bytes, and once pitch, tempo, or vocal removal are applied the YouTube stream is no longer what plays. Showing video would mean downloading and storing video files or keeping a muted embedded player in sync with processed audio, which drifts as soon as tempo changes. Presto plays audio only, and the lyrics screen is the visual. This is a permanent scope decision, not a deferral.
